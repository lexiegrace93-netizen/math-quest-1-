How Math Quest works

Each mission is designed for about 10 minutes of focused practice. Students must answer each question correctly before moving on. Hints and worked examples provide support without blocking retries.

What the score means

  • First-attempt accuracy: questions correct before any retry ÷ questions completed.
  • Completion: questions completed ÷ questions assigned.
  • Retries and hints: counts of support needed during the mission.

This is a practice tool aligned to the FASTbridge plan's focus areas. It does not replace the recommended CBMmath Automaticity progress-monitoring assessment.
